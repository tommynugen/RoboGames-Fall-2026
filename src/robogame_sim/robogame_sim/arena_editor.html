Arena layout

Place

  • Click or drag to place. The peg holes are evenly spaced across the floor tiles, so modules go where the pointer is, to the nearest half square, and can stand across two tiles.
  • Walls and thwomps take one row of two holes, on the line of holes under the pointer, so two fit on a tile side by side, or one each way.
  • Click a ramp, wall or thwomp with its own tool to turn it a quarter turn; R turns the one under the pointer.
  • Shift-click, or turn on Stack, to put a module on top of platforms: a ramp, wall, thwomp or another platform, across two if they are as high. The corner shows how high the top is.
  • Right-click to take the top module off.
