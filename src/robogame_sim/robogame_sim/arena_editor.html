Place

Help
  • The Place cards are in groups, guessed from their names: click a group's name to fold it. Drag a card to move it, into another group too, or right click it to type its group. The number keys follow the order, which this browser keeps.
  • Click or drag to place. The peg holes are evenly spaced across the floor tiles, so modules go where the pointer is, to the nearest half square, and can stand across two tiles.
  • Walls and thwomps take one row of two holes, on the line of holes under the pointer, so two fit on a tile side by side, or one each way.
  • Floor tiles go on whole squares, under whatever is there. Deleting a tile takes what stands on it too.
  • Click a ramp, wall or thwomp with its own tool to turn it a quarter turn; R turns the one under the pointer.
  • Shift-click, or turn on Stack, to put a module on top of whatever is there: anything stacks on anything. It rests on the highest top under it. The corner shows how high the top is.
  • Right-click to take the top module off, or press X for delete mode, where clicks and drags take modules off; X again or Esc to go back.
  • Press P for paint mode: clicks and drags give the top module the colour beside Paint, right-clicks its own colour back. The sim builds it in that colour too.
  • Drag an STL file onto the page to add it as a module of its own. It is a block over the 2 inch cells under it, which robots go round, and is saved next to the layouts in parts.
  • The 3D view edits the same way: click or drag on a tile or a module's top. Right-drag turns the view, middle-drag moves it and the wheel zooms.
