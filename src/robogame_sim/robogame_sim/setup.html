Gets the real arena ready, step by step. Each step checks itself every 2 s and turns green when done; the blue one is next. Done steps fold away: click Show to open one again. The tab named on the right of a step has the rest of its controls.

  1. 1

    Build

    Runs colcon build --symlink-install. Needed after a pull or an arena_perception copy; skip it if nothing changed.

    Upkeep tab
  2. 2

    Start the BLE bridge

    Connects the real robots by Bluetooth. Switch them on first.

    Robot console tab
  3. 3

    Pair controllers

    Hold Create and PS on each controller until its light flashes, then Find controllers and Pair.

    Robot console tab
  4. 4

    Camera and arena size

    Point the camera down at the arena so it sees the corner tags 1 to 4, and give the arena's side between them.

    Vision tab
  5. 5

    Map robot tags

    Say which tag is on which robot. Tags the camera sees that are not corners or mapped yet are listed with a Map button.

    Vision tab
  6. 6

    Assign controllers

    Runs arena_perception's assign_controllers for these robots: press a button on each controller in turn, as it asks on the Logs tab under Teleop.

    Robot console tab
  7. 7

    Go

    Robots the console sees. Drive them with their controllers, or make them autonomous and send them places on the Robot console.

    Robot console tab
